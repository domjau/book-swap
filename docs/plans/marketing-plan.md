# seku seku – launch and marketing plan

Last updated: 2026-11-15. Owner: Dominykas. Editable version (Claude Docs): https://claude.ai/artifact/Q7pCVoqDn64YEbxrzGMWPr

Launch at one school first, put 100+ books on the shelf before inviting anyone, hold a real-life swap before Christmas, then grow school by school through Lithuanian communities in January. Almost everything is free.

## 1. The message
- **One-liner:** Children's books, passed from family to family. **LT:** Vaikiškos knygos – iš šeimos į šeimą.
- Three promises, in this order: **Meaningful** (books your kids outgrew are exactly what another family's kids are ready for – every book is loved twice) · **Easy** (scan, snap, done in under a minute) · **Free and close by** (no fees, no ads, no password; families nearby, in your language).
- **Tone:** warm, short, parent to parent. Real photos of books, never children's faces. Always lowercase *seku seku*. Lithuanian first for the Lithuanian community, English for everyone else.
- **Story:** a parent at the Lithuanian Saturday school in Copenhagen noticed how hard it is to find Lithuanian children's books abroad, while every family has a shelf of books their kids have outgrown.

## 2. Launch plan, week by week
Books come before people: an empty shelf kills a swap app.

**Week 1 (16–22 Nov) – get ready**
- [ ] Finish Phase 0 of the production plan (email codes, notifications, contact email)
- [x] Privacy notice, delete account and 18+ checkbox live (Claude) – in PR #6; live after merge + migration 007
- [ ] Reserve @sekuseku (or similar) on Instagram and Facebook
- [ ] Pick 5 founding families (teachers and active parents)

**Week 2 (23–29 Nov) – fill the shelf**
- [ ] You + founding families add 15–20 books each: 100+ books before inviting anyone
- [ ] Founding families add a wish list each
- [ ] Fix whatever confused the founding families

**Weeks 3–4 (30 Nov–13 Dec) – pilot at the Saturday school**
- [ ] Message in the parents' group
- [ ] Poster with QR code at school
- [ ] Two-minute demo at pick-up
- [ ] A helper at school who adds books with parents

**Mid-December Saturday – Christmas book swap at school**
- [ ] Swap table: bring books, add them on the spot, take others home
- [ ] Photos of the table and books (no faces) for social posts
- [ ] One-sentence quotes from 3–5 parents

**Late December – listen**
- [ ] 5-question feedback form to pilot families
- [ ] Fix the top three problems

**January – public launch ("new year, lighter shelves")**
- [ ] Lithuanian community in Denmark and other Lithuanian Saturday schools in Scandinavia
- [ ] Facebook groups for Lithuanians in Denmark and Scandinavia
- [ ] Spanish-speaking parent groups in Copenhagen
- [ ] Press note to Lithuanian diaspora media

**February–March – school by school**
- [ ] One new school or community a week, each with a "seku seku friend" who answers questions and runs a swap table once a term

## 3. Channels
| Channel | Who it reaches | What to do | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saturday-school parents' group | Pilot families | Launch message, then one post a month | Free |
| Swap table at school | Parents who skip group chats | Once a term, QR poster on the table | Free |
| Poster + QR code | Pick-up, libraries, Lithuanian church or club | A4 poster | ~100 kr |
| Instagram / Facebook page | Wider diaspora | 2 posts a week | Free |
| Facebook groups for Lithuanians abroad | Families in DK and Scandinavia | One launch post, then real stories only | Free |
| Other Lithuanian schools | Teachers, parent committees | Personal email, offer a visit or call | Free |
| Lithuanian community organisations | Newsletters and events | Short article, swap table at events | Free |
| Diaspora media | Lithuanians worldwide | Press note with founder story | Free |
| Spanish-speaking parent groups | Second language community | Spanish post and poster | Free |

Post as yourself, not a brand page – parents trust a parent. Check each group's rules first.
