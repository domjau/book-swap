# seku seku – brand summary

Full guidelines (design system): https://claude.ai/artifact/GnSn6pP4DWjciLT3t8L57S

- Name: always lowercase **seku seku**, in the logo and in text, even at the start of a sentence; short form **seku**. Descriptor: BOOK SWAP.
- Logo: the single-line shelf (one standing book, one leaning), from logo concept no. 5. The app's welcome screen shows the mark tile with "seku seku" under it.
- Palette B "Summer meadow": cream #F7F3E8 (ground), paper #FFFDF8 (cards), sage #5C7A61 (brand, primary buttons with white text), sage-deep #43604A (links, focus), honey #E3C16F (highlights, ink text only), rosehip #C77B6A (warm accent), ink #2E2E2A (text), muted #66625A.
- Tints: sage-tint #E4EBE2, honey-tint #F6EBCB / honey-deep #7A5B14, rose-tint #F3E0DA / rose-deep #8E4A3D.
- Type: Outfit 400/500/600 (Google Fonts, latin-ext). No weights above 600.
- Shape: 4px grid, radii 12 (controls) / 18 (cards) / 22 (sheets), 1.5px borders, 2px round-cap icons, one small shadow on covers only.
- Files: `brand/` folder in domjau/book-swap (logos, icons, social, email, tokens.css); email templates in `supabase/email-templates/`.
