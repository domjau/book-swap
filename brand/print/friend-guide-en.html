You are the school's seku seku friend

Thank you! You help families at your school swap children's books. It takes about an hour a month. Here is everything you need.

Your job in one line

Be the friendly face who helps parents add their first book and answers questions.

Before the first swap

Add 10–15 of your own books, so the shelf isn't empty. Ask 3–5 active parents to do the same. Put the poster with the QR code up at school.

Helping a parent sign up (3 minutes)

Scan the QR code → enter email → type the 6-digit code from the email → name, country, postal code → tick the two boxes. Then: Add → scan the barcode → take a photo → save.

Swap table, once a term

A table at pick-up with the poster. Parents bring books, add them on the spot, and take others home. Take photos of the table and books – never of children.

Common questions

Is it free? Yes, no fees, no ads. Do I give my address? No, only a postal code – you meet at school. Book not found? Type the title; the next scan will find it.

Safety

Handovers happen in public places, ideally at school. Never pay in advance to strangers. Children don't get accounts. Report anything odd with the flag button – admins look at it.

Something wrong?

Write to the seku seku admins (contact in the app: Profile → Terms → Contact). Tell us what confused parents – we fix it.
